A Neighborhood Shop Since Way Back

A small bakery, with a taste neighbors never forget.

Lyncourt Bakery is a homestyle bread shop in the Lyncourt neighborhood of Syracuse. We believe in keeping things simple: bread baked from scratch, donuts fried fresh every morning, and every customer welcomed like a neighbor.

Nothing fancy here — just consistent, homemade flavor, every day starting at 6 AM.
